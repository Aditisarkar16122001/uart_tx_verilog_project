# UART Transmitter (Verilog)

FSM-based UART transmitter (8N1, LSB first) written in Verilog and
verified with a self-checking testbench in Xilinx Vivado.

## Files
- uart_tx.v: UART transmitter (states: IDLE, START, DATA, STOP)
- tb_uart_tx.v: self-checking testbench
- uart_tx_waveform.png: simulation waveform
- uart_tx_pass_log.png: console output (all tests passed)

## Test
Sent 5 bytes (0x41, 0x55, 0xA3, 0xFF, 0x00). The testbench samples the
tx line in the middle of each bit and compares it with the sent byte.
All 5 passed.

## Parameters
CLKS_PER_BIT = clock frequency / baud rate
(100 MHz / 115200 = 868; the testbench uses 10 for a fast simulation)
